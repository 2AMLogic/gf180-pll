- **Result**:

  ### 0. Does the ring oscillate at all, at every point on the grid?

  A current-starved ring must satisfy Barkhausen with the small-signal
  gain its cell actually has: an N-stage single-ended ring needs a
  per-stage gain of 1/cos(pi/N) -- **2.00 for N=3**, 1.24 for N=5, 1.11
  for N=7. A current-starved inverter loses gain as its head and tail
  devices are driven fully on, because their series resistance degenerates
  the stage. So the top of the band is where a low stage count is at risk,
  and that is exactly where this grid finds it:

  | Stages | required per-stage gain | points where the ring did not start | verdict |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | 2.00 | 1 of 48 | **DISQUALIFIED** |
  | 5 | 1.24 | 0 of 48 | oscillates everywhere |
  | 7 | 1.11 | 0 of 48 | oscillates everywhere |

  The 3-stage ring is **static** (max = min on the measured ring
  node across the whole window, despite the same start-up kick that
  dislodged the other counts) at: all-fast B7 @ 0.9 V.
  This is a measured outcome, not a missing measurement -- see the
  `fosc_hz = 0` convention in `stage_count.csv`.

  ### 1. Can each stage count reach the band edges at the binding corner?

  Top of band is binding at the **slow** corner (all-slow), bottom of band at
  the **fast** corner (all-fast) -- the ring has to still be fast enough at one
  extreme and still be slow enough at the other.

  | Stages | max f at all-slow (B7, Vctrl 2.7 V) | vs 200 MHz | min f at all-fast (B0, Vctrl 0.9 V) | vs 10 MHz |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | 550.2 MHz | PASS (+175 %) | 11.41 MHz | **FAIL** (+14 %) |
  | 5 | 256.6 MHz | PASS (+28 %) | 6.633 MHz | PASS (-34 %) |
  | 7 | 182.5 MHz | **FAIL** (-9 %) | 4.739 MHz | PASS (-53 %) |

  ### 2. Cost at a matched frequency

  Supply current of each complete ring (bias generator included) at the
  band/Vctrl point closest to 100 MHz at the nominal corner (typical), which is
  where the draft power line is quoted:

  | Stages | closest point to 100 MHz | f | I_supply | energy per output cycle |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | B5 @ 0.9 V | 93.8 MHz | 104 uA | 3.65 pJ |
  | 5 | B6 @ 0.9 V | 90.4 MHz | 146 uA | 5.31 pJ |
  | 7 | B5 @ 2.7 V | 91.79 MHz | 212 uA | 7.62 pJ |

  Energy per cycle is the comparable number across counts (current alone
  is not, because the three rings are not at the same frequency). Relative
  to the 5-stage ring: 3 stages 0.69x, 5 stages 1.00x, 7 stages 1.43x.

  ### 3. Swing at the bottom of the band

  A current-starved ring loses output swing before it loses oscillation;
  a ring node that no longer reaches the rails cannot drive a
  rail-to-rail buffer reliably at every corner. Worst-case swing over the
  whole grid, as a fraction of that run's supply:

  | Stages | worst swing (fraction of vdd) | at |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3 | 0.680 | all-fast, B6, Vctrl 2.7 V |
  | 5 | 1.006 | all-slow, B0, Vctrl 0.9 V |
  | 7 | 1.006 | all-slow, B0, Vctrl 0.9 V |

  ### 4. Conclusion

  | Stages | starts everywhere | reaches 200 MHz slow | reaches 10 MHz fast | energy/cycle | worst swing | verdict |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | **no** | yes | **no** | 3.65 pJ | 0.680 | **rejected** |
  | 5 | yes | yes | yes | 5.31 pJ | 1.006 | viable |
  | 7 | yes | **no** | yes | 7.62 pJ | 1.006 | **rejected** |

  **5 stages is confirmed** as DR-001 Decision 2's nominal count, and
  it is the *only* viable odd count of the three: each of the other two
  is rejected by a measurement, not by a preference.

  - **3 stages -- rejected**: **it does not start at 1 of 48 grid points** (all-fast B7 @ 0.9 V) -- with a per-stage gain requirement of 2.00 it cannot sustain oscillation once the starving devices are driven fully on, and it then sits at mid-rail drawing static crowbar current; and its lowest band floors at 11.41 MHz at the fast corner, **14 % above the 10 MHz bottom of the band** -- it cannot reach the bottom of the ratified range without the output divider DR-002 Decision 2 keeps out of v1 scope; and its worst-case ring swing falls to 0.68 of the supply, so the output buffer would not see a reliable full-swing input at every corner.
    (Its 3.65 pJ per output cycle, 0.69x the 5-stage ring, is not the reason;
    the failures above are disqualifying on their own.)
  - **5 stages -- viable**: clears the 200 MHz v1 ceiling at the slow corner by 28 %, reaches 34 % below the 10 MHz floor at the fast corner, starts at every grid point, keeps full swing (1.01 of supply worst case), and costs 5.31 pJ per output cycle.
  - **7 stages -- rejected**: it tops out at 182.5 MHz at the slow corner, **9 % short of the 200 MHz v1 ceiling** DR-002 Decision 2 budgets margin to.
    (Its 7.62 pJ per output cycle, 1.43x the 5-stage ring, is not the reason;
    the failures above are disqualifying on their own.)

  This **confirms** rather than revises DR-001 Decision 2, so it is not
  grounds for a superseding decision record on the stage count. It does
  narrow the decision: DR-001 left the count open as "odd (5 nominal)",
  and this grid shows the neighbouring odd counts are not fallbacks --
  there is no second choice to retreat to if layout parasitics erode the
  5-stage margins, which makes the extraction step (#18) the point where
  this decision would actually be at risk.
