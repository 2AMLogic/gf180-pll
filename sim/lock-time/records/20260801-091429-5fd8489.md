    settled, when the window ends). There is no discontinuity, glitch, or
    sign reversal anywhere in the trace.
  - **Conclusion this waveform supports, directly answering the open
    question from `eec269e`**: at `ss`/125 C/2.97 V, `relock`'s behavior is
    **not** the `up`-dominant, past-the-clamp rail excursion the `typical`
    corner showed -- it is the **opposite and textbook-expected** pattern:
    `dn` dominates (consistent with starting above the 80 MHz operating
    point, since at 2.7 V band 5 already runs well above 80 MHz at this
    corner too -- `sim/vco-tuning-range`'s own worst-margin-corner framing
    for `ff`/125 C/2.97 V does not directly give the `ss` Kvco number, but
    the same monotonic-band-vs-Vctrl relationship holds across process),
    and `vctrl` moves correctly toward the target rather than away from it.
    **This answers the `eec269e` open question at this one additional data
    point: the large-frequency-error anomaly is NOT a universal property of
    every corner at N=4/band 5/`relock`** -- it is corner-dependent, seen at
    `typical`/27 C/3.30 V and not seen (in this one additional sample) at
    `ss`/125 C/2.97 V. This is evidence toward, not proof of, a
    corner-dependent mechanism: **one additional corner is not the full
    grid**, and a mechanism that is absent at `ss` but present at `typical`
    could still recur at some of the 43 still-unsampled corners, including
    ones closer to `typical` than `ss` is. The FAIL status itself is
    unchanged from the pilot -- LOCK did not assert in either corner's 2 us
    window -- but the FAIL's *character* is materially different: at `ss`
    it is an ordinary "window too short, loop still converging correctly"
    FAIL (the same story the pilot's own `cold` row told), not the
    rail-excursion anomaly `relock` showed at `typical`.
