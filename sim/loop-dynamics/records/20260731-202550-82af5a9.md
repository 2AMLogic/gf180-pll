- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - **Exact reduction, not an approximation.** For the type-II charge-pump
    loop of DR-001 Decision 1, `T(s) = (Icp/2pi)*Z(s)*(2pi*Kvco/s)/N =
    A*Z(s)/s` with `A = Icp*Kvco/N`, so Icp, Kvco and N enter ONLY through
    the scalar A. One measured filter impedance curve per (passive corner,
    temperature, Vctrl) point therefore covers the ENTIRE Kvco x N x Icp-trim
    x f_ref cross-product for that filter corner exactly, rather than by
    interpolation or a monotonicity argument -- see `analyze.py`'s module
    docstring for the derivation. The A axis is still swept on a dense
    (25-point) log grid between its measured extremes, not just
    evaluated at the extremes, so no unimodality assumption is relied on
    either.
  - **Independent cross-check.** `tb_loop_ac.sp` puts the real filter inside
    a phase-domain behavioural model of the WHOLE loop (charge pump as an
    Icp/2pi A/rad gain, VCO as an ideal phase integrator, divider as 1/N) and
    lets ngspice compute `T(jw)` directly at real (Icp, Kvco, N) points
    `pick_xcheck.py` selects from #8's/#9's measured tables -- one point per
    reference-frequency decade x the three extreme filter corners. Section 8
    of the Result reports the agreement between this independent simulation
    and the impedance-plus-algebra result.
  - **Criteria applied** (stated up front, not left implicit): phase margin
    >= 45 deg everywhere in the operating space (this record's own adopted
    criterion -- DR-001 states none); `f_c < f_ref/10`, the sampled-loop
    stability ceiling DR-001 Decision 1 names explicitly; small-signal 1 %
    settling < 100 us (draft lock-time target, 20 us stretch); C1 area
    reported against the draft 0.15 mm^2 block budget.
  - **R/C1/C2 are not drawn values here, they are MEASURED** from each Z(jw)
    curve's own asymptotes and phase peak (a 3-element fit), so process/temp
    variation on the real devices is what actually drives the swept filter
    corner, not a separate hand-scaled R/C.
  - **Band-selection rule is load-bearing (DR-003 Decision 4).** Kvco is
    taken under the rule that a correctly configured part selects the LOWEST
    band that reaches the target f_out; #8's record separately reports an
    adversarial-band-choice Kvco that exceeds DR-001's ~150 MHz/V ceiling, and
    that adversarial case is NOT part of this record's cross-product (it is
    not a legal configuration).
  - **Lock-time is a small-signal estimate**, from the fitted 3-element
    model's dominant closed-loop pole -- cycle slipping and the VCO's
    large-signal nonlinearity during cold-start acquisition are #12's number,
    not this record's; stated as a limitation, not silently extrapolated.
  - **Schematic-level, no parasitics.** Routing/coupling parasitics on the
    filter's own R/C network are not in this record; #18 (extraction) is
    where those enter.
  - Statistical switches: `sw_stat_global = sw_stat_mismatch = 0` (nominal
    per-corner skew, no Monte Carlo) -- inherited from the underlying #4/#8/#9
    records this campaign consumes.

  ### 7. Control-line ripple vs. lock time

  The dominant ripple mechanism in lock is **not** the UP/DN current
  mismatch during the anti-backlash window -- at #9's measured 4.7 % and a
  ~1 ns reset pulse that is under 1 fC per cycle.  It is #9's measured
  per-event CHARGE asymmetry: |q_up + q_dn| = 2.16 fC median, 3.68 fC worst
  (`sim/cp-compliance`, worst at `fs/125C/3.63V Vctrl=0.9`).  In lock the loop nulls the average
  by standing off a static phase offset t_os = dQ/Icp = 0.735 ns, and the
  charge packet lands on C2 before redistributing into C1 through R.

  | Quantity | Worst-corner dQ | Median dQ |
  |---|---|---|
  | Vctrl ripple, peak (dQ/C2) | 1.825 mV | 1.07 mV |
  | Peak instantaneous df/f (kappa = 0.5/V) | 0.0912 % | 0.0535 % |
  | Bound on the TIE it can accumulate | 0.6693 ps | 0.2303 ps |

  The trade is explicit and it is C2's alone: ripple scales as 1/C2, the
  pole-zero spread that buys phase margin at the top of the f_ref range
  scales as C1/C2.  `ripple_tradeoff.csv` sweeps C2 through the fitted
  3-element model of the measured filter -- only the as-built row is
  simulated, the others substitute an ideal C2 into the fit, and both
  model columns are evaluated at the TYPICAL filter corner only, so the
  cell count is a trend indicator and NOT comparable with section 4's
  corner-swept verdict:

  | C2 | Vctrl ripple, worst dQ | worst PM (typ-corner model) | cells passing (of 140) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0.5 pF | 7.357 mV | 34.2 deg | 134 |
  | 1 pF | 3.678 mV | 34.0 deg | 134 |
  | 2.02 pF (as built) | 1.825 mV | 33.5 deg | 132 |
  | 4 pF | 0.9196 mV | 31.6 deg | 110 |
  | 8 pF | 0.4598 mV | 22.7 deg | 86 |

  Which side of that trade binds is set by the CHARGE PUMP, not by this
  filter, and against the charge pump as it is measured today it is the
  phase-margin side: worst-corner ripple at the as-built C2 is 1.825 mV
  (0.0912 % instantaneous df/f), and even at 1 pF it is only 3.678 mV,
  for +0.45 deg of worst-case model phase margin; going the other way to
  4 pF costs 22 of the 140 cells.  The as-built 2.02 pF sits on the
  flat part of the phase-margin curve, and it is not ripple-limited.

  **Lock time.** For f_c well above the loop's zero the closed loop is
  over-damped and its slowest pole sits near 1/(2*pi*R*C1) = 17.09 kHz, so
  settling saturates at about 43 us however fast the loop is made -- more
  Icp does not help past that point.  Of the 140 (f_ref, N, code) cells,
  120 meet the <100 us draft target and **0 meet the <20 us stretch**.

  DR-001's hand calc predicted exactly this ("lock times land in the tens
  of us, meeting the <100 us target but not the <20 us stretch at the
  low-ref end"); it is now measured rather than asserted.  These are
  small-signal settling estimates from the fitted model -- cycle slipping
  and the VCO's nonlinearity make cold-start acquisition #12's number, not
  this record's.

  ### 8. Independent check: the whole linearized loop, simulated

  `tb_loop_ac.sp` puts the real filter inside a phase-domain model of
  the whole loop (charge pump as Icp/2pi A/rad, VCO as an ideal
  integrator, divider as 1/N) and lets ngspice compute T(jw) directly.
  Over 18 such runs, spanning the recommended trim codes and the
  extreme filter corners, the simulated crossover and phase margin agree
  with the impedance-plus-algebra result of section 4 to within
  **0.000 % in f_c and 0.000 deg in phase margin** -- see
  `loop_ac_crosscheck.csv`.

  ### 9. Sanity checks on the measurement itself

  - |Z|/w strictly decreasing (so the crossover is unique) on 486 of 486
    measured curves.
  - Crossover inside the swept 100 Hz - 100 MHz band at every evaluated
    point: yes.
  - 3-element fit vs. measured |Z|, worst error over all curves and all
    frequencies: 0.13 %.

  ### 10. Verdict

  **Overall: PASS on the contracted operating space, FAIL off it.**

  The filter meets the 45 deg phase-margin criterion and stays under the
  f_c < f_ref/10 sampled-loop ceiling at **every** corner of the
  cross-product once the trim code follows the rule in section 6.  The trim code is not a free margin knob: it is the
  loop's reference-frequency compensation, and section 6 is the rule that
  has to travel with these component values -- exactly as DR-003's
  band-selection rule travels with the Kvco table.
