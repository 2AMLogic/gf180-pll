- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - **Ruling out (1), the measurement-window artefact.** `lock_hi` averages
    `v(lock)` over `p7..p8` only (the last 3.2 us of the 40.08 us hold). If
    the detector re-asserted earlier in the hold and the *window* simply
    missed it, the retained FULL-hold trace
    (`supply_transient_<corner>_esc.csv`, decimated to 20 ns/sample, covering
    the entire hold from the step edge onward) would show a `LOCK` excursion
    toward the rail somewhere between the step and `p7`. It does not, at any
    of the 3 corners: excluding the ~200 ns around the step edge itself
    (`LOCK` tracks the moving `VDD` rail exactly for the duration of the
    100 ns edge -- capacitive coupling to the rail transition, not an
    assertion; visible in the trace as `LOCK == VDD` for those samples, and
    excluded here from `5.9 us` onward), `|LOCK|` never exceeds
    1.8e-4 V anywhere in the 5.9 us..40.1 us window at any of the
    3 corners (`lock_plateau_max_v` below) -- five to six orders of magnitude
    below a 0.90-of-rail assert level. **The flag is not chattering into and
    out of assertion inside the hold; it stays down for the whole of it.**
    `lock_hi`'s own value (already in `46b92f8`, re-quoted below) is
    therefore representative of the entire plateau, not an artefact of where
    its averaging window happens to sit.
  - **Distinguishing (2) from (3): the same quantity, three ways.** The
    record's own Methodology (`46b92f8`, section 2) establishes
    `w_up - w_dn == the REF->FB skew` -- the static phase error at
    `lock_detector`'s own UP/DN inputs, read at a different node. `phi07`/
    `phi08` (`tb_supply_dyn.sp`'s REF->FB phase measurement at the high
    -plateau window's two edges, already computed by the escalated run) is
    that same quantity, sampled 3.2 us apart inside the plateau. Comparing it
    against two independent references, both already committed and neither
    re-derived here:
    1. `sim/lock-detector`'s own measured window
       (`window_edges.csv`) at the matching process/temperature -- the
       largest phase error that still lets the detector assert, and the
       smallest that does not. This is what decides "is the flag's window
       fundamentally too narrow for this phase error, independent of how it
       got there."
    2. **This same corner's own undisturbed steady-state phase and LOCK
       verdict, at the SAME 3.63 V rail** (`supply_steady.csv`, read
       directly -- `lock_lvl_v`, not the single-priority `verdict` column;
       see the caveat below). This is the control condition: if the detector
       asserts fine on this corner once the loop has actually settled, a
       dynamic-hold failure at the same corner and the same final voltage is
       a settling question, not a structural one.
  - **Caveat on `supply_steady.csv`'s `verdict` column.** `report.sh` writes
    one verdict per row from an `else if` chain in priority order
    (`ferr` > `phi` > `N`/`o` > `lock`, `report.sh` lines 212-217), so a row
    that fails BOTH the phase check and the LOCK check is reported only as
    `FAIL:phi` -- it does not appear in `46b92f8`'s own "LOCK flag below 0.90"
    breakdown list, which is built from that same single-valued column. That
    list is therefore a lower bound on LOCK failures, not an exhaustive one.
    `ff`/125 C/3.63 V is exactly such a row: `verdict=FAIL:phi`, and its own
    `lock_lvl_v` (5.564e-07 V, column 17) shows the LOCK flag was
    *also* deasserted -- consistent with the phase-error-driven mechanism
    below, not a contradiction of `46b92f8`. This record reads the raw
    `lock_lvl_v` column directly rather than the verdict label for exactly
    this reason.
  - **Limitation -- `sim/lock-detector`'s window is not characterised at every
    voltage this record needs.** `window_edges.csv` measured `ss`/-40 C at
    3.63 V (an exact match to this record's high-plateau rail) but `ff`/125 C
    at 2.97 V and `typical`/27 C at 3.30 V -- not 3.63 V. The window is stated
    to be an absolute inverter-chain delay (`spec/pll.md`, "Lock detector":
    "the window is an absolute number of nanoseconds... a fixed time, not a
    fixed fraction of a cycle"), which says it should not move with the
    reference period, but neither that record nor this one measures its
    supply dependence directly. Where this record cites a window value for
    `ff` or `typical`, that mismatch is real and stated, not glossed over;
    `ss`, the corner with the largest gap to close (see Result), is not
    affected by it.
  - **Limitation -- no finer time resolution than the retained trace's
    20 ns/sample decimation, and no direct UP/DN pulse-width measurement in
    this analysis.** `phi07`/`phi08` (REF->FB edge skew) are used as the
    equivalent of the UP/DN width skew rather than measuring UP/DN directly,
    per the equivalence `46b92f8`'s own Methodology already establishes; no
    new `.meas` was added and no new simulation was run to obtain a direct
    UP/DN reading at these three additional instants (see #387 for the
    loop-dynamics question this raises, where a fresh transient with finer
    per-cycle instrumentation would be scoped work, not a redo of this
    analysis).

  **`ff`/125 C/3.63 V -- (2), a structural window/phase mismatch, already
  documented at the spec level.** This corner's OWN undisturbed steady state,
  at the SAME 3.63 V rail, does not assert LOCK either: `lock_lvl_v` =
  5.564e-07 V (`supply_steady.csv`, `ff,125,3.63`), with a static
  phase of 1.796 ns -- past `lock_detector`'s own measured window
  edges at the nearby `ff`/125 C corner (1.2-1.5 ns at 2.97 V) and past the
  spec's ratified Lock criterion (<= 1 ns). No amount of additional settling
  time changes this: the corner's fully-settled phase is already outside the
  window. This is a **new, independent confirmation** of `spec/pll.md`'s
  already-documented Lock detector gap ("T1/T2 ... the design does not meet
  them today ... a correctly locked part may fail to assert `lock`. The fix
  is geometric (widen the delay window, or scale the integrating capacitor),
  not architectural") and of DR-007 Amendment A1's carve-out of row 16 from
  ratification for exactly this contradiction -- found here from a
  completely different campaign (a large supply STEP) and a different
  corner's exact operating point than `sim/lock-detector`'s own 95-point grid
  characterised. It is not a new design question; it is more evidence for an
  already-open one, filed as design issue #387 because no live issue
  currently tracked the T1/T2 fix itself.

  **`ss`/-40 C and `typical`/27 C -- (3), a transient phase-settling tail,
  new evidence for `sim/loop-dynamics` (#10).** Both corners assert LOCK fine
  in their OWN undisturbed steady state at the identical 3.63 V rail
  (`ss`: phase 0.9232 ns, well inside `lock_detector`'s own 1.0-1.2 ns
  window measured at this EXACT corner and voltage; `typical`: phase
  0.9422 ns, inside its 1.2-1.5 ns window). So the mechanism is not
  structural at these two corners -- the detector demonstrably CAN assert
  here. What differs after the step is that `phi07 -> phi08` is still
  decaying at the escalated hold's own end (`typical`: 2.668 -> 1.886 ns,
  -0.24 ns/us; `ss`: 3.211 -> 2.298 ns, -0.29 ns/us), well above
  each corner's own settled value, 34.4 us (3.7 tau) after the step edge
  ends. `ferr_hi` -- a drift-RATE proxy over that same 3.2 us window -- has
  already converged under its own looser 1e-3 threshold while the
  ABSOLUTE phase has not yet returned inside `lock_detector`'s much tighter
  window (nor inside the spec's ratified 1 ns bound): the loop's phase
  settles on a materially slower tail than its frequency-drift proxy
  suggests. This is the SAME kind of finding `46b92f8` section 3c already
  routed to #10 for `ss`/-40 C's END plateau (`under-damped`, decay ratio
  0.809 against a 0.0493 single-pole expectation) -- this record extends it
  to the HIGH plateau, and to `typical`/27 C as well, which 3c's END-plateau
  escalation had classified `settles` (not under-damped). `ss`/-40 C remains
  the corner furthest from settled: its steady value (0.9232 ns) sits
  further below its window's lower edge (1.0 ns) than either other corner,
  meaning it has more phase to shed before LOCK can assert, consistent with
  it also being 3c's worst under-damped corner.
