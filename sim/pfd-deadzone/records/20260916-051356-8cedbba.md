- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - **Same DUT, same measurement, different operating point.** The DUT (`pfd_cp`), the stimulus topology, the ideal bias references, the 200 ps edges, the Icp trim code (b1 b0 = 10, 3 unit legs) and every solver option are `testbench/tb.json`'s, unchanged. Exactly three things differ, and they are the variables #394 asks about: `period` is 80 ns (12.5 MHz) instead of 40 ns; `vctrl` is a declared sweep axis over 0.90 / 1.65 / 2.40 V instead of one point at 1.65 V; and the charge-integration window is expressed relative to `period` instead of as a literal. Per sim/README.md ('Two manifests, one experiment directory') this stays in the `pfd-deadzone` directory rather than becoming a sibling slug, because it is the same DUT measured a second way.
  - **The reported quantity is `t_offset` = -q_zero / kd_wide**, the static phase offset at the PFD inputs that nulls the pump's residual per-cycle charge -- the same reduction `testbench/derive.py` computes, so the two records' numbers are directly comparable. `q_zero` is the net charge delivered per reference cycle at dphi = 0 and `kd_wide = [q(+1ns) - q(-1ns)] / 2ns` is the detector gain well outside the reset window.
  - **The dphi axis is the wide pair plus zero, and nothing else.** The near pair (+/-200 ps) exists in the parent manifest to test LINEARITY through zero -- the dead-zone claim. That claim is the parent's and is already answered (45/45 corners pass, worst ratio 1.13); re-testing it at a second operating point is not this record's question, and the three points here are exactly what `t_offset` needs. No dead-zone verdict is computed or claimed by this manifest.
  - **f_ref = 12.5 MHz is not a weaker choice than the parent's 25 MHz -- it is a different one.** 25 MHz is the TOP of DR-002 Decision 1's ratified 1-25 MHz range, where the PFD's reset-and-recover window is the largest fraction of the reference period, and it is the demanding end for a dead-zone claim. 12.5 MHz is the reference `sim/supply-sensitivity`'s closed loop actually runs at (100 MHz / N = 8), which is the only reason it appears here. A `t_offset` difference between the two manifests at the same corner and the same 1.65 V is therefore a measurement of the f_ref dependence, with every other variable held.
  - **The control-window ladder is DR-001 Decision 2's own window, not a convenience triple.** 0.90 V and 2.40 V are the stated ends of the ~0.9-2.4 V usable control window (the same bounds `sim/supply-sensitivity`'s `ACC_VCTRL_LO`/`ACC_VCTRL_HI` apply to its closed-loop rows); 1.65 V is its middle and the parent manifest's single point, retained here so the f_ref comparison above has a held-constant control voltage to run through.
  - **The charge-integration window is period-relative** -- `from='tstart+period' to='tstart+3*period'` rather than the parent's literal `from=60n to=140n`. It integrates the same two whole reference cycles (the 2nd and the 3rd; the 1st is discarded as PFD start-up, both latches powering up reset) at either period, and reproduces the parent's `qnet2` bit-for-bit (-6.99339e-15 C) at the parent's own 40 ns / 1.65 V / `ff`/125 C/3.63 V settings.
  - Transient: 3 reference cycles at 80 ns = 240 ns of signal, run to 280 ns, 20 ps maximum timestep, `reltol` 1e-4 / `abstol` 1e-14 -- the parent manifest's controls, with only the stop time extended to cover the longer period.

A closed-loop static phase offset that exceeds `t_offset` at the loop's own operating point is therefore evidence that something other than the pump's residual charge is setting it -- which is precisely the inference #394 needs, and why the open-loop isolation is retained rather than relaxed.
