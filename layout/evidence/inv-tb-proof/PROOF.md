## Supplementary: the tap-necessity control

Not part of the four checks above (run separately via
`python3 layout/run_pv.py bare-cell`, not regenerated by `prove`): streaming
the bare `gf180mcu_fd_sc_mcu9t5v0__clkinv_1` standard cell out **without**
the tie/fill row `inv_tb.gds` carries produces exactly two DRC violations,
`DF.13_MV` and `DF.14_MV` (NCOMP-in-nwell / PCOMP-outside-nwell too far from
a well/substrate tap) — confirming `layout/harness/cell.py`'s documented
claim that a bare standard cell is not, and is not expected to be, DRC clean
by itself. This artifact is not committed (regenerate on demand; it is a
supplementary note, not part of the pass/fail proof).
