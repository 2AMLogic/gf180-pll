- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Measurement topology: two bias generators are instantiated side by side at every PVT point, from the same ideal rails and the same static band code -- `design/netlist/vco.spice`'s own `.subckt vco_bias` (as specified, `ppolyf_u_3k`) and `vco_bias_as_drawn_350.spice`'s `.subckt vco_bias_as_drawn_350`, which is that same subckt with exactly one token changed per resistor (`ppolyf_u_3k` -> `ppolyf_u`) by `make_counterfactual.py`. Three copies of each, at Vctrl = 0.9 / 1.8 / 2.7 V (the bottom, middle and top of the usable control window), each through its own supply ammeter. The copies share only the supply node and ground.
  - The ring is NOT in this deck. What the ring sees is measured on replicas of `vco_stage.sch`'s own two bias-driven devices -- the tail `MNT` (nfet_03v3 W=4 um L=0.5 um, gate on VBN) and the head `MPH` (pfet_03v3 W=10 um L=0.5 um, gate on VBP) -- with their drains held at mid-rail, carrying the identical ad/as/pd/ps/nrd/nrs geometry expressions the schematic gives them. `i_tail` is therefore the per-stage starving current the 5-stage ring is biased to, i.e. the quantity f_osc is proportional to, read at a DC operating point instead of from an oscillating transient. It is a bias-network measurement and is not a substitute for `sim/vco-tuning-range`'s own measured f(Vctrl).
  - Four witness resistors (`ppolyf_u_3k` and `ppolyf_u`, each at the drawn W=1 um and both drawn lengths L=5.6 um / 33 um, 1.0 V across each) measure the resistance the corner actually delivers, so every current in this record is reported alongside the resistance behind it rather than a quoted model parameter. `rsh_ratio` is the measured 3k/unmarked resistance ratio at the RCG geometry -- the 8.6x figure issue #381 states, measured rather than assumed.
  - `ivco_*_100m_ua` sums the bias generator's own supply current and 5x the replica tail current. The replica tail is held at mid-rail, i.e. in saturation, so 5x it is the ring's PEAK starving budget, not its cycle-average supply current (a switching stage's tail spends much of each cycle with its drain well below mid-rail). It is an upper bound on the ring term, used here only to compare the two arms against each other and against the ratified domain figure's order of magnitude; `sim/vco-tuning-range` remains the source of the measured per-copy VCO supply current.
  - Analysis is a DC operating point (`.op`), so the numbers are the bias network's own settled state. The constant-gm core has two DC solutions (the zero-current state and the operating one) and carries `vco_bias.sch`'s own start-up branch (MSU1/MSU2/MSU3) to make the zero state non-sustaining; `itail_a_mid_ua`'s `min` check below fails loudly if any point nevertheless lands in the zero state, rather than that point being recorded as a very low bias current.
  - No absolute pass/fail is placed on the AS-SPECIFIED arm, deliberately. spec/pll.md#power's 318 uA `vdd_vco` figure is the worst of 159 grid points at a fixed OUTPUT FREQUENCY (90 <= f_osc <= 110 MHz), reached at a corner-dependent band code and Vctrl; this deck holds the operating POINT fixed (band 5, Vctrl 1.8 V -- spec/pll.md's own named 100 MHz reference point) and lets f_osc move with the corner, so at `res_ff`/125 C the same point is a far faster oscillator than 100 MHz and legitimately draws more. A `max` check on the as-specified arm would therefore be testing the corner's frequency, not the bias network. What IS point-independent -- and is checked -- is that the counterfactual arm exceeds the entire ratified domain figure at EVERY point of the grid, by 6.3x at its own best corner. The nominal-corner comparison against 318 uA is reported in the record and in layout/evidence/vco-layout/PROOF-381-high-rs-resistor.md rather than asserted as a check.
  - The band-select code is a sweep axis with two points (band 0 = 000 and band 7 = 111). The band code does not touch the constant-gm reference at all -- it switches the band mirror legs downstream of VBP0 -- so it is swept at its two extremes rather than all eight, which is the axis that changes VBP/VBN and therefore the replica currents.
  - Corner axis: the full 13-bundle set, because the claim rides directly on the resistor family (`res_ff`/`res_ss` move the high-sheet resistance by roughly -35%/+40%) and on MOS (the beta multiplier's own reference current is a MOS-and-resistor quantity, not a resistor-only one). Temperature -40/27/125 C and supply 3.30 V +/-10% are the mandated axes, unmodified.
  - Statistical switches: sw_stat_global = sw_stat_mismatch = 0 (nominal skew, no Monte Carlo). This is a corner-matrix claim; RCG's own mismatch contribution to the reference current is not in scope here.
  - Limitation: the counterfactual arm is a *diagnostic*, not a design alternative. It exists so the bias-current consequence of the drawn device class can be measured instead of asserted, and it must not be cited as a proposed sizing.
