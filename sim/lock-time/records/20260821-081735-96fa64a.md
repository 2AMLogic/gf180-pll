  - **Lock criterion, cold-start/re-lock mechanism, design point, simulator
    settings, PFD DN-branch integration guard**: all identical to

  - **Host contention, measured (this session, not a historical citation)**:
    `uptime` was checked repeatedly across this session (2026-08-21,
    ~06:20-08:20 UTC) and consistently read a sustained load average of
    ~60-78 on this 8-core host, driven by a combination of (a) a sibling
    repo's own concurrent `ngspice` campaign
    (`gf180-ldo/.loom/worktrees/issue-51`, unrelated to this repo, observed
    cycling through several of its own testbenches during this window) and
    (b) -- per the fleet-dispatch comment on issue #65 at
    2026-08-21T07:13:21Z -- "several other concurrent Loom sweeps ... running
    simulation/build workloads on the same shared host". A bounded
    wait-and-recheck loop (8 checks, ~70s apart) at the start of this session
    showed load oscillating between ~7 and ~78 rather than trending down, so
    the contention is bursty and driven by other sessions' own campaigns
    restarting, not a one-off spike expected to clear on its own.
  - **A direct measurement of the cost this contention imposes, taken this
    session**: an attempt at the nominal corner (`typical`/27C/3.30V) N=16
    **cold** point -- the "at minimum the nominal corner first" step #65's
    own checklist names as the reasonable first slice of the N=16/64 item --
    did **not** complete. After **11m46s of wall-clock time** and **38m35s of
    accumulated ngspice CPU time** (a single-process CPU/wall ratio of ~3.3x,
    itself evidence of contention -- an uncontended run would show close to
    1x for this DUT) the transient had not finished advancing past its
    `Initial Transient Solution` print, and the run was terminated by this
    session rather than left to consume further, unbounded compute with no
    visible progress. **This is not reported as an `ERROR` row anywhere in
    this record or its CSV** -- unlike a genuine simulator-reported failure
    (a named node/timestep, as `sim/output-range`'s `sf`/27C rows show) or an
    `output-range`-style `SIM_RUNCAP` budget termination, a session-side kill
    of a run that has not reached any verdict is neither, and reporting it as
    one would misattribute an artifact of this session's own time budget to
    the design. It is disclosed here in prose, as the concrete evidence for
    why the N=16/64 grid item remains unstarted, not folded into the CSV.
  - **Per-run cost actually measured this session** (each row's own
    `Total analysis time`, from the archived log under `corners/`): `typical`
    /27C/3.30V/cold 138.7 s, `/relock` 505.3 s; `typical`/-40C/2.97V/cold
    399.1 s, `/relock` 63.5 s; `ss`/27C/3.30V/cold 141.4 s, `/relock` 220.5 s;

    seven rows (63.5 s to 921.9 s, a ~14.5x range at the SAME N and window
    length) is itself further evidence that wall-clock cost here is
