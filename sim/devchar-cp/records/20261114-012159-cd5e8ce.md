- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Measurement topology: each stack's output drain is tied through its own 0 V ammeter to a single swept node `sw`, shared by all six stacks; one DC sweep of `sw` from 0 to Vdd in 10 mV steps traces the complete output I-V of every stack in one analysis -- through the saturation knee and into triode, not just the flat region. A second `op` analysis records each polarity's reference-device operating point (vth / vdsat / gm / gds), because the compliance numbers are only interpretable next to the inversion level they were taken at.
  - ro(V) = |dVout/dIout| is the same +/-10 mV central difference on the 10 mV sweep grid the pre-migration extraction used, re-expressed as a pair of `.measure dc find ... at=` reads and a `param=` quotient; the P-polarity stacks carry an explicit leading minus because their output current falls as Vout rises. ro is reported at 0.3, 0.5 and 0.7 x Vdd, as before.
  - Compliance is reported as a *knee ladder* rather than as the pre-migration record's single crossing voltage: chd0v20 / chd0v40 / chd0v60 / chd0v80 are Iout normalised to the stack's own mid-rail current, read at 0.20 / 0.40 / 0.60 / 0.80 V of headroom from the rail the stack works against (ground for the N sinks, Vdd for the P sources). The pre-migration `vcomp_i1pct` / `vcomp_i5pct` are the inverse function of this ladder -- the headroom at which the ratio reaches 0.99 / 0.95 -- and are read off it by interpolation. The change of form is forced, not preferred: ngspice's `.measure ... when <var>=<value>` cannot take a value derived from another measurement (`when i(vam_ns)='0.99*imid_ns'` fails at netlist-parse time with 'Undefined parameter'), so a threshold defined relative to a measured mid-rail current is not expressible as a manifest measurement. The ladder carries strictly more information than the two scalars it replaces.
  - `abstol=1e-16 reltol=1e-7 vntol=1e-9 gmin=1e-15` (the pre-migration deck's `.option` card, verbatim): the output-resistance extraction differences currents of order 10 pA over a 10 mV step, which the default abstol=1e-12 would otherwise dominate. Do not trust any ro above ~10 MOhm without this.
  - Sizing: NMOS W=5um, PMOS W=15um, L=1um (deliberately long-channel analog geometry, not the digital minimum-length devices in devchar-delay); cascode bias diode at W/4; Iref=20uA, chosen so both polarities sit in moderate inversion (Vdsat ~ 0.2 V) at Iref -- the regime a charge pump actually uses.
  - Statistical switches: sw_stat_global = sw_stat_mismatch = 0 (nominal device skew, no Monte Carlo). CP mismatch is a separate campaign (#15, mc-cp-mismatch), not this record.
  - LIMITATION -- one pre-migration column family and one artifact are NOT reproduced here. `vcomp_ro50` / `headroom_ro50` (the output voltage at which ro falls to 50% of its mid-rail value) requires walking the swept curve for a threshold crossing of a *derived* quantity, and `cp_curves.csv` is the decimated curve itself; the harness records scalar per-point measurements, so neither is expressible in a manifest today.
