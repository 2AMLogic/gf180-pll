- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - **The question, stated so the answer cannot be over-read.** "Does the
    loop re-lock after the step" is not answerable from a run that ended
    while the phase was still moving. What IS answerable is: *given the
    phase remaining at the hold's end, the rate it was moving at, and the
    loop's own dominant time constant, how much more settling does that
    distance represent?* Every column here answers that narrower question.
  - **Two extrapolations, both reported, neither preferred.**
    - `t_extra_linear_us` -- the remaining phase divided by the mean decay
      rate measured across the `p7..p8` window. A decaying response slows as
      it converges, so a straight-line continuation of the *current* rate is
      the **optimistic** end of the bracket.
    - `t_extra_singlepole_us` -- the same distance closed by a single
      exponential at the loop's own dominant `tau` = 9.313 us. The asymptote
      is not assumed: it is solved for from the two committed samples and
      that `tau` (`implied_asymptote_ns`). This is a two-parameter fit to two
      points, with all the fragility that implies -- it is reported because
      the loop's own dominant pole is the one physical model available from
      ratified data, not because two samples determine it.
  - **One model-free column**, so a reader can see what was observed with no
    model at all: `ratio_to_assert_meas` = `(phi08 - a)/(phi07 - a)`, the
    shrink factor of the distance to the detector's assert edge across one
    3.2 us window. Compare it to `k_singlepole` = `exp(-dt/tau)` = 0.7092.
    Both settling-tail corners come in **below** it (0.4674 and 0.5872) --
    over the window actually observed, the distance to the assert edge was
    closing **faster** than one pole at the loop's own `tau`, not slower.
  - **Limitation -- and it is the one that decides this record's verdict.**
    `20260901-155456-46b92f8` section 3c measured the **END**-plateau
    *frequency* residual at this same `ss`/-40 C corner decaying by a factor
    0.809 over 3 `tau` against a 0.0493 single-pole expectation -- about 16x
    slower than one pole -- and classified it `under-damped` on that basis.
    That is the opposite sense to `ratio_to_assert_meas` above. The two are
    not measuring the same thing (different plateau, and frequency residual
    vs. absolute phase) and 3c's own record hedges its resolution ("
    `under-damped` and `under-resolved` are different claims"), so this is
    not a contradiction that can be resolved here -- but it is exactly the
    situation in which a single-pole extrapolation must not be promoted to a
    result. **If the post-step phase tail behaves like 3c's END-plateau
    frequency residual rather than like the window observed here, both
    bracket ends understate the answer by a large factor.**
  - **Limitation -- two samples.** `phi07`/`phi08` are the only two phase
    samples the escalated run committed on this plateau. The retained
    transient (`supply_transient_<corner>_esc.csv`) carries `LOCK`, not a
    per-cycle phase, so no third point is available to test the decay law
    without re-simulating.
  - **Limitation -- the detector's own assert latency is NOT included** in
    `t_relock_*_us`. Those columns are when the *phase* re-enters the assert
    edge, not when the flag comes up. `lock_detector`'s assert time from deep
    in lock is a further 0.680 .. 1.913 us
    (`sim/lock-detector/records/20260802-050119-c24ee3a.md`, worst at
    `ss`/125 C/2.97 V), which adds on top. Kept separate rather than summed
    so each term stays traceable to the record that measured it.
  - **Limitation -- 3 corners of 45, and one supply excursion of one size.**
    The step is 3.30 -> 3.63 V (+10 % of nominal) with a 100 ns edge, at
    f_ref = 12.5 MHz / N = 8. Nothing here generalises to a different
    excursion size, a different edge rate, a different (f_ref, N, trim-code)
    cell, or the 42 unsampled corners.

  **1. The hold was short, not the loop slow.** Both settling-tail corners'
  phase re-enters the detector's assert edge between **37.2 and 42.6 us**
  after the step edge -- 8 % to 24 % past the 34.40 us the escalated run
  reached. Adding `lock_detector`'s own 0.680 .. 1.913 us assert hold-off
  (cited separately, deliberately not summed into the table) puts the flag's
  re-assertion in the neighbourhood of **38 .. 44.5 us**. For scale, the same
  loop's *cold-start* structural settling floor is **43 us** and its worst
  small-signal 1 % settling under the trim rule is **71 us**
  (`spec/pll.md` "Lock time", DR-006 Decision 7). **A post-step phase
  recovery landing at or below the cold-start floor is the expected
  behaviour of this filter, not a defect in it.**

  **2. Nothing here indicts DR-006's sizing.** DR-006 sized the filter
  against a steady-state phase-margin criterion (>= 45 deg, met at 47.4 deg
  worst) and a settling floor set by the dominant pole. Both settling-tail
  corners' observed decay over the window measured is *faster* than that
  dominant pole would give (`ratio_to_assert_meas` < `k_singlepole` at both),
  which is the wrong sign for "the filter is under-damped enough to matter
  here". The evidence available does not support a design change, and this
  record does not propose one.

  **3. But this is a bracket, and it is not enough for a spec number.**
  `46b92f8` section 3c's `under-damped` END-plateau result at the same
  `ss`/-40 C corner is inconsistent with the single-pole model both bracket
  ends rest on (Methodology). Two phase samples cannot distinguish the two
  behaviours. **What is MEASURED, and all that is measured, is a lower
  bound**: at 2 of the 3 sampled corners, `lock` had not re-asserted
  **34.40 us (3.70 tau)** after a +10 % supply step, and the phase was still
  decaying monotonically at that instant. Everything past that instant in
  this record is extrapolation, labelled as such in every column heading.

  **4. `ff`/125 C is not in this comparison at all.** Its own undisturbed
  steady state at the same 3.63 V rail does not assert `lock`
  (`lock_lvl_v` = 5.564e-07 V), so no amount of settling time changes its
  verdict. It is finding 1's structural corner, addressed by
  `sim/lock-window-sizing` and the T1/T2 decision, not by this one.
