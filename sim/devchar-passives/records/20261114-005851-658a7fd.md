- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Measurement topology: a linear voltage ramp 0 -> 3.63 V over 1 us drives a shared node; each of 10 candidate devices is tied to it through its own 0 V ammeter. The ramp current of a branch is i = dq/dt = (dq/dv)(dv/dt), so C(V) = i / (dv/dt) is the true differential (small-signal) capacitance the simulator itself uses, independent of how the model expresses its charge. dv/dt is measured from the simulated waveform (t_v330 - t_v030 over 3.0 V), not assumed, exactly as the pre-migration extraction did. After the ramp the node is held at 3.63 V for a further 1 us; with dv/dt = 0 the residual branch current is pure leakage (`leak_*`, read at t = 2 us).
  - Geometry: every device drawn 30 x 30 um = 900 um^2, so densities (dmin/dmax, fF/um^2) are directly comparable. dmin/dmax and the C-V linearity ratio cvr = dmax/dmin are taken over a 0.30 .. 3.30 V control window (i.e. the ramp between t = 92.6446281 ns and t = 919.090909 ns), which excludes the last 300 mV against each rail.
  - Corner axis: mimcap_* and moscap_* are INDEPENDENT axes from the MOS tt/ff/ss/fs/sf sections, and every device here depends on exactly one of them, so stepping them together still gives every device its complete min/typ/max envelope. The harness bundles `typical` / `all-fast` / `all-slow` carry exactly (mimcap_typical + moscap_typical) / (mimcap_ff + moscap_ff) / (mimcap_ss + moscap_ss), which is the lockstep sweep the pre-migration runner performed. Supply is not an axis: the 0 -> 3.63 V ramp is itself the voltage sweep and already covers the control range at the top of the 3.3 V +/-10% supply range. Both omissions must be declared on the command line, e.g. `python3 sim/run_corners.py devchar-passives --subset-reason 'capacitor-only DUT: mimcap_*/moscap_* stepped together are the governing process axis; the 0..3.63 V C-V ramp is the voltage axis, supply is not'`.
  - `extra_lib_sections: [cap_mim]` pulls in the legacy-name MIM subcircuits (`cap_mim_1f0fF` / `_1f5fF` / `_2f0fF`) of sm141064.ngspice. No corner bundle includes that section, so without the key those three device names would not resolve; including it lets the two MIM naming families be compared in the same deck, as the pre-migration deck did with an explicit `.lib cap_mim`. The section is corner-independent -- it reads the mim_corner_* params the mimcap_* section sets -- so adding it at every point cannot pin a corner-varying axis.
  - Solver: abstol=1e-18, reltol=1e-7, vntol=1e-9, gmin=1e-15 (the pre-migration deck's `.option` card, verbatim). Leakage in these models runs from femtoamps (cap_mim_2f0, the only one with a leakage element) down to identically zero, so the solver has to be tightened well past its defaults for the leakage column to mean anything.
  - Statistical switches: sw_stat_global = sw_stat_mismatch = 0.
  - Limitation: this record reports scalar C-V points and window extrema, not the archived C(V) curve the pre-migration runner decimated into cap_cv.csv. The curve for record 20260731-041345-833292f remains committed under that record's corners/ directory.

Two families of column differ by more, and the cause is the extraction, not the devices. (1) c_0v30 differs by up to 1.27 % -- and only for the MOS caps, whose C(V) is climbing steeply at 0.30 V (the archived curve shows C rising ~4.8x between 0.204 V and 0.386 V, ~0.9 %/mV). The pre-migration awk took the ramp sample NEAREST 0.30 V on a <=3.6 mV grid; `.measure tran find i(...) when v(r)=0.30` interpolates at exactly 0.30 V, so a <=1.5 mV grid offset accounts for the whole difference, and the interpolated value is the more accurate of the two.
