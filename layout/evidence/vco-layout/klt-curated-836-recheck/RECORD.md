# Curated-deck LVS on `vco_block`, with the decap in the reference (issue #855)

Re-run of the curated-deck (`deck: gf180mcu`) LVS recorded in
`../klt-curated-836/`, with the two request options and the reference form the
diagnosis in issue #854 identified. Single-unit local run; no corner grid.

## Inputs

- Layout: `../vco_block.gds` (unchanged).
- Reference: `vco_block-decap-xcard.spice`, generated by
  `vco.block.reference_netlist(decap_as_subckt_call=True)`. It differs from the
  committed `../decap-20261009/vco_block.spice` in exactly one line (81): the
  decap card `C_XCDEC1 ...` is emitted as `X_XCDEC1 ...` (same terminals, model,
  W/L/M), which is the form `klt lvs --reference.form subckt-call` can parse.
- Request: `lvs-request.json` -- `deck_options.poly_res: "3k"`,
  `options.combine_devices: true`, `reference.form: subckt-call`.

## Result (`klt 0.8.1.dev969+gf6d4cd522990`)

```
klt lvs --format json lvs-request.json > lvs-report.json
klt lvs --check lvs-report.json     # all checks OK, exit 0
```

- `status: match`, `error_count: 0`.
- Nets 43/43 matched, devices 66/66 matched.
- 5 warnings, 0 errors: 2 x `device.geometry_not_compared`, 2 x
  `device.placeholder_value`, 1 x `topology` (class `bjt` on neither side;
  not a real mismatch).

## What this does and does not establish

- The `device.placeholder_value` warnings mean the capacitor `C` and the three
  resistors' `R` were **not compared** (reference values are the literal 0
  placeholder of a subckt-call conversion), and the `geometry_not_compared`
  warnings mean `A`/`P`/`L`/`W` were not compared either. **Resistor and
  capacitor values are unverified by this run.**
- Device and net counts and connectivity match; it does not exercise the #837
  strap, since the LVS joins same-named nets.
- This is a curated-deck result; it does not replace or alter the
  foundry-deck `match` in `../lvs-clean/`. The earlier
  `../klt-curated-836/` mismatch record is unchanged.
